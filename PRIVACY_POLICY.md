## Data Protection Measures

### Pseudonymization
We employ **pseudonymization** as a core privacy protection:
- Your birth data is assigned a unique code identifier (e.g., "A10001")
- Your real name is NOT stored in our digital systems
- The mapping between codes and identities is maintained separately in offline records
- This ensures that if our digital systems are compromised, your identity remains protected

### Technical Security
- **API Key Authentication:** All calculation requests require secure authentication
- **Encryption in Transit:** All data transmission uses HTTPS/SSL encryption
- **Self-Hosted Infrastructure:** We operate our own private calculation servers
- **No Data Retention:** Calculation requests are not permanently stored on our servers
- **Access Controls:** Only authorized personnel can access systems

### Physical Security
- Identity mapping records are maintained in secure physical storage
- Access is restricted to authorized personnel only
- Records are not digitized or uploaded to any cloud service
