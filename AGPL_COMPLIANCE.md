## FAQ

**Q: Can I charge $100/hour for readings using this?**
A: YES! Commercial use is allowed.

**Q: Do I have to give away my client charts?**
A: NO! Only source code must be shared, not client data.

**Q: Can someone steal my code and compete with me?**
A: They can use the code (that's the point of open source), but they:
- Can't use your business name
- Don't have your client relationships
- Don't have your expertise
- Must also keep their version open source

**Q: What if I add a cool new feature?**
A: Anyone using your service can request that code too. But your expertise, client relationships, and service quality are what make your business valuable - not secret code.

**Q: Do I need to tell every client about AGPL?**
A: Best practice: Add a link to your GitHub repo on your website privacy page or terms of service.

**Q: What if I want to close-source it later?**
A: You'd need to purchase Swiss Ephemeris Professional License from Astrodienst (costs money) and remove all AGPL code.
