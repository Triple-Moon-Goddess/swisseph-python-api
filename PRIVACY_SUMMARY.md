## How We Protect You

### 🔐 Your Identity is Protected

**You are assigned a unique code (like "A10001")**
- Your real name is NOT stored in our digital systems
- The connection between your code and name is kept separately in secure, offline records

This is called **"pseudonymization"** - the same privacy technique used by hospitals and medical facilities.
